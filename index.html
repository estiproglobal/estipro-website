Straight answers

Questions contractors ask us first

Do I really pay nothing upfront?

Correct. We start the work with zero upfront payment and send you preview files. You only pay once you've seen the preview and you're happy with it. After payment, we send the full Excel and source files.

How fast can you turn an estimate around?

It depends on the size and trades, but we move on your bid date. Send us the plans and the deadline, and we'll tell you honestly whether we can hit it before you commit.

Are you replacing my estimator?

No. Think of us as your backup or overflow estimator. Use us when your own team is buried, when a bid lands at a bad time, or when you want a second set of eyes on the numbers.

What software and formats do you work in?

We deliver color-coded PDF markups and editable Excel takeoffs that drop straight into your bidding process. If you have a preferred format or template, tell us and we'll match it.

What does it cost?

One flat price per project, agreed before we start. No hourly billing and no surprise add-ons. Send the plans and we'll quote it.
