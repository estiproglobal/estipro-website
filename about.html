Who we are

Estimating, minus the headache

EstiPro Global does construction cost estimating, quantity takeoffs, and bid support for general contractors and subcontractors across the United States. When your own estimator is buried, when a bid lands at the worst time, or when you just want a second set of eyes — that’s us.

We keep it simple on purpose. Send the plans, get a flat quote, see a preview, and only pay if the work is good. No retainers, no long contracts, no hourly meter quietly running.

Our job is to make your bid faster and cleaner, then get out of your way.
