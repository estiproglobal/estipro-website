Overview

This document is a starting template for EstiPro Global and is provided for general informational purposes. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified attorney before publishing to make sure it fits your business and complies with the laws that apply to you.

This Privacy Policy explains what information EstiPro Global (“EstiPro Global,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects when you use our website or request an estimate, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, email us, or upload plans, we collect details such as your name, company, email address, phone number, project documents, and anything you include in your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we may collect basic technical data such as your browser type, device, and pages visited through standard logs or analytics tools.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to review your project, prepare and deliver your quote and estimate, communicate with you, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Project documents and confidentiality

Plans and drawings you send are used only to prepare your estimate. We do not share your project documents publicly or with unrelated third parties. Representative examples used in our portfolio never include confidential client drawings or names.

Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate (for example, email or file-hosting tools), only as needed to deliver our services, and with anyone you direct us to. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal or business requirements, then delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing estimating@estiproglobal.com. You can opt out of marketing emails at any time.

Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the “last updated” date above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email estimating@estiproglobal.com.
