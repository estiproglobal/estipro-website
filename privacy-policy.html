This Privacy Policy explains what information EstiPro Global collects when you use our website or request an estimate, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you request a quote, email us, or upload plans, we collect your name, company, email, phone, project documents, and anything you include.

Collected automatically. Basic technical data such as browser, device, and pages visited via standard logs or analytics.

How we use it

To review your project, prepare and deliver your quote and estimate, communicate with you, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Project documents

Plans you send are used only to prepare your estimate. We never share confidential drawings publicly or with unrelated third parties.

Sharing

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate (email, file hosting), as needed, and with anyone you direct us to, or if required by law.

Retention & your choices

We keep information only as long as needed, then delete or anonymize it. Email estimating@estiproglobal.com to access, correct, or delete your data, or to opt out of marketing.

Cookies

Our site may use cookies or similar technologies to understand usage. Control them in your browser settings.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the date above will change. Questions? estimating@estiproglobal.com.
